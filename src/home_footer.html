Frequently asked questions

What should I paste into the checker?

Paste the job description and the instructions for applying. Include any requests from the recruiter about payments, equipment, or documents. You can add the title, requirements, and benefits separately.

What does a green tick tell me?

The classifier found low-risk wording and no warning rule triggered. To verify the opening, look for the role on the employer’s own careers page or contact the employer directly.

Why was this ad flagged?

A warning can come from a suspicious language pattern or a specific request, such as an upfront fee. Open the screening result to inspect the matched text and the words that influenced the classifier.

How can I keep a screening result?

Reopen the ad in your session’s history and download its HTML report for reading or its JSON report for analysis. Refreshing the page starts a new session.
